03 / COMPLEX TECHNICAL CASES

Problems solved.
Systems brought back.

A closer look at the kind of difficult, layered technical issues I work through — where symptoms can be misleading, several components may be involved, and every step needs careful verification.

01

HARDWARE DIAGNOSTICS / BOARD-LEVEL FAULT ISOLATION

Dead systems & complex motherboard faults

The challenge: A laptop may show no power, no display, unstable charging or repeated shutdowns — symptoms that can come from different points in the power and motherboard circuits.

How I approach it: Start with visual inspection and symptom isolation, check adapter and power-input behaviour, inspect relevant board sections, trace likely power-rail or charging faults, and narrow the fault to a component or circuit area before attempting repair.

Verification: Re-test startup, charging, display, thermal behaviour and connected functions after repair instead of relying on a single successful power-on.

↗
02

FIRMWARE / BOOT CHAIN / OS RECOVERY

Systems that fail before Windows even starts

The challenge: A machine can become unbootable because of firmware settings, damaged boot records, UEFI/Legacy mismatches, storage detection issues, corrupted system files or failed updates. The visible error is not always the root cause.

How I approach it: Separate firmware-level symptoms from OS-level faults; verify BIOS/UEFI configuration, boot mode, boot entries and drive detection; then use suitable recovery media and targeted repair steps. For BIOS recovery or programming, verify the exact model and firmware before proceeding.

Verification: Confirm repeatable boots, correct drivers, stable updates and normal operation after deployment — including consistent setup across multiple systems.

↗
03

STORAGE FORENSICS / DATA-PRESERVATION WORKFLOW

RAW, inaccessible & undetected SSD / NVMe drives

The challenge: A drive may appear as RAW, disappear intermittently, fail to mount or remain invisible through an enclosure. Repeated repair attempts can make recovery harder if they write to the original media.

How I approach it: Check drive detection at the device and system level, validate enclosure and interface compatibility, inspect partition and file-system symptoms, and distinguish connection/controller issues from logical corruption. Prioritize read-only assessment and create a clone or image when practical before recovery attempts.

Verification: Check recovered data for readability and completeness where possible, and clearly distinguish recoverable files from cases that need specialist lab-level recovery.

↗
04

NETWORK / CCTV / LIVE ENVIRONMENT SUPPORT

Keeping connected school and office systems operational

The challenge: Connectivity complaints can involve cabling, power, IP configuration, router settings, camera links, recording devices or endpoint configuration. A fault in one layer can look like a failure somewhere else.

How I approach it: Trace the connection path layer by layer — physical link, power, addressing, gateway and device configuration — then isolate whether the issue sits with the endpoint, network equipment or CCTV path. Check camera feeds, recording status and remote access settings within the authorized environment.

Verification: Test access from the intended user location, confirm stable connectivity and recording, and document any remaining limitations for follow-up.

↗
05

CROSS-PLATFORM / END-USER SYSTEM RESTORATION

MacBook, printer, deployment & custom PC troubleshooting

The challenge: Peripheral and platform issues often combine driver conflicts, startup behaviour, OS configuration, connectivity, component compatibility and user workflow requirements.

How I approach it: Reproduce the fault, isolate hardware from software causes, validate drivers and interfaces, check component compatibility during PC assembly, and use controlled setup steps for printers and multi-system deployments.

Verification: Confirm the device works in the real user workflow — printing a test page, detecting peripherals, completing startup and passing basic stability checks.
