02 / CAPABILITIES

Technical expertise.

01
⌘

Laptop & Desktop Hardware Repair

Triage difficult and sometimes intermittent laptop and desktop faults by separating hardware, firmware, operating-system and peripheral causes. Perform component-level checks for RAM, storage, power delivery, display and thermal issues; narrow down the failure point before repair or part replacement, then validate system stability.

02
⌬

Motherboard & Chip-Level Repair

Work beyond basic part swapping: inspect motherboard symptoms, trace power and charging-related faults, test relevant circuit sections and identify suspect components. Carry out chip-level repair where appropriate, using a diagnostic-first approach and post-repair checks to confirm that the board and connected systems behave correctly.

03
▣

Windows Installation & OS Troubleshooting

Handle full OS deployment and difficult Windows recovery cases, including boot failures, corrupted system files, driver conflicts, failed updates and recurring crashes. Prepare bootable media, troubleshoot installation blockers and configure multiple systems consistently, followed by driver, activation and operational checks.

04
⌁

BIOS / UEFI & Boot Recovery

Investigate failures that occur before the operating system loads: firmware settings, boot modes, UEFI entries, boot order, corrupted boot configuration and BIOS-related startup faults. Support compatible BIOS recovery and programming workflows with careful model and firmware verification to reduce the risk of an unusable system.

05
◈

SSD / HDD Data Recovery

Approach inaccessible, RAW, undetected and unstable HDD/SSD/NVMe devices as data-preservation cases first. Assess device detection, connection/enclosure compatibility and file-system symptoms; choose recovery steps carefully, avoid unnecessary writes to the source drive and verify recovered files wherever possible.

06
▤

Data Backup & Drive Cloning

Plan backup and cloning operations for drive upgrades, migrations and failing-storage scenarios. Check source health, capacity, partition structure and destination selection before imaging; validate the resulting data or bootability and keep the original source protected during the process.

07
⌘

Networking & Router Configuration

Troubleshoot connectivity across routers, LAN, Wi-Fi and endpoint devices by checking physical links, addressing, gateway/DNS configuration and device-level causes. Isolate whether a fault sits with the endpoint, cabling, router or wider network, then restore and verify reliable access for users and systems.

08
◉

CCTV Installation & Support

Handle CCTV setup and fault-finding across cameras, cabling, power, recording equipment and network access. Trace no-video, intermittent-feed and connectivity problems through the signal path, check configuration and recording status, and verify that cameras and recording systems are functioning after intervention.

09
▧

MacBook & Printer Support

Diagnose MacBook hardware and macOS issues with attention to device-specific symptoms, startup, power and peripheral behaviour. Also resolve printer deployment problems spanning drivers, USB/Wi-Fi connectivity, queue errors and print quality, confirming successful operation from the user’s workstation.

10
⌑

Authorized Password Recovery & Security Testing

Support legitimate access recovery and permission-based security assessments on systems owned by the organization or explicitly authorized for testing. Verify scope and authorization first, use approved recovery procedures, document access issues responsibly and protect credentials and user data; no unauthorized account access or bypassing.

03 / SELECTED WORK

Problems solved.
Systems restored.

01

Dead-System Diagnostics & Board-Level Fault Isolation

Investigate laptops that show no power, intermittent startup or abnormal charging behaviour. Trace faults through the adapter input, charging and power-management stages, inspect board-level components, isolate likely short or open circuits, and carry out chip-level repair where appropriate. Finish with controlled power-up, thermal observation and stability checks.

↗
02

Firmware, BIOS/UEFI & Multi-System OS Recovery

Work through complex boot failures involving firmware settings, corrupted boot configuration, incompatible drivers and damaged operating-system installations. Diagnose whether the fault is firmware, storage, bootloader or OS related, restore the appropriate layer, then validate startup, device drivers and repeatable deployment across multiple systems.

↗
03

Unstable Storage, RAW Volumes & NVMe Data Preservation

Assess drives that appear intermittently, show RAW partitions or fail to mount through an enclosure or host system. Check detection and health indicators before selecting a recovery path, protect the source from unnecessary writes, create a sector-level clone when feasible, and verify recovered files or migrated data without overstating recoverability.

↗
04

Network, CCTV & Infrastructure Fault Tracing

Troubleshoot connectivity problems across routers, switches, endpoints and CCTV recording paths. Follow the signal from physical cabling and power through IP addressing, network reachability and device configuration; isolate whether failures originate at the endpoint, network layer or recorder, then verify camera feeds, recording and access after the fix.

↗
05

Cross-Platform Restoration & Peripheral Integration

Resolve hardware and startup issues across MacBooks, custom-built PCs and Windows systems, alongside printer driver, queue and connectivity faults. Separate software symptoms from component failures, confirm compatibility and configuration, and perform end-to-end checks so the repaired device, peripheral or workstation is ready for normal use.
